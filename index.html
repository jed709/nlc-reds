Prices are CAD. World is Wine-Searcher's global average retail price converted to CAD; markup compares the NLC shelf price with HST stripped against that ex-tax world average, so a negative number means the NLC is cheaper than the world. Canada is the lowest other Canadian retail price Wine-Searcher lists.

Critic is the mean of professional critic scores Wine-Searcher lists for that exact vintage (count in parentheses); CellarTracker is excluded from it. CT is CellarTracker's community score for the exact vintage with the number of tasting notes; where only Wine-Searcher's copy of the CT score was available it is shown as a whole number without a count.

SALE marks bottles the NLC currently discounts; the struck-through price is the regular shelf price and the saving is the NLC's own figure. Markup is computed on the current (sale) price.

A vintage marked est. is not stated on the NLC listing; the most recent vintage on the market with real retail offers is assumed. CT unrated means the bottle is on CellarTracker but nobody has scored that vintage; the most recent scored vintage is shown after it as context only and never counts as the wine's rating. When the NLC states a vintage, a rating from another vintage is never substituted; for unstated vintages CellarTracker uses its most recent reviewed vintage, shown after the note count when it differs. New marks wines the NLC tags as just arrived or that first appeared in a recent scrape.

In stock only (on by default) hides bottles the NLC lists as out of stock online or that no store currently has; switch it off to see everything, including clearance items already sold through.

Region, appellation and grape are derived from the listing name, the bottle label, and the Wine-Searcher and CellarTracker names with a keyword table; classed-growth Bordeaux is placed by château. The grape for a blend is the appellation's typical blend. Unrecognised wines fall back to country and the NLC's own varietal tag.

thin after a markup means Wine-Searcher shows fewer than three listings for that vintage, so the world price is a weak reference and the markup is shown without colour.

Formats: halves, litres, magnums and double magnums are included when their price per 750 mL is $30 or more; the world price and markup are scaled to the bottle's size, and the per-750 price is shown beside the shelf price. Boxes and casks are excluded.

Stores shows the NLC's own per-store counts from each product page at the time of the last update; pick a store at the top to see only what that store has on the shelf (your choice is remembered on this device).
