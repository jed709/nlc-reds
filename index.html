Prices are CAD. World is Wine-Searcher's global average retail price converted to CAD; markup compares the NLC shelf price with HST stripped against that ex-tax world average, so a negative number means the NLC is cheaper than the world. Canada is the lowest other Canadian retail price Wine-Searcher lists.

Critic is the mean of professional critic scores Wine-Searcher lists for that exact vintage (count in parentheses); CellarTracker is excluded from it. CT is CellarTracker's community score for the exact vintage with the number of tasting notes; where only Wine-Searcher's copy of the CT score was available it is shown as a whole number without a count.

SALE marks bottles the NLC currently discounts; the struck-through price is the regular shelf price and the saving is the NLC's own figure. Markup is computed on the current (sale) price.

A vintage marked est. is not stated on the NLC listing; the most recent vintage on the market with real retail offers is assumed. A rating from a different vintage is never substituted. New marks wines the NLC tags as just arrived or that first appeared in a recent scrape.

In stock only (on by default) hides bottles the NLC lists as out of stock online or that no store currently has; switch it off to see everything, including clearance items already sold through.

Stores shows the NLC's own per-store counts from each product page at the time of the last update; pick a store at the top to see only what that store has on the shelf (your choice is remembered on this device).
