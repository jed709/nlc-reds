Theme mock-ups

Same data and layout, different palettes. Each opens the full site in that theme.

  • Paper — Warm cream, claret accent, serif title (current).
  • Cellar — Charcoal and gold, dark by default.
  • Claret — Deep burgundy header band, white cards, red-wine palette.
  • Slate — Cool neutral greys with a blue accent, sans-serif throughout.
  • Olive — Muted green and sand, earthy and low-contrast.
